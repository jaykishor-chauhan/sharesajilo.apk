### 📱 About Sharesajilo

> 🇳🇵 **Sharesajilo** (शेयर सजिलो) is an all-in-one, feature-packed mobile application engineered for Nepalese stock market investors, traders, and everyday share market enthusiasts.
> 
> From **live NEPSE price movements** and **floorsheet analysis** to **MeroShare IPO allotment checks** and **smart portfolio tracking**, Sharesajilo simplifies stock market management into a clean, modern interface.

<br />

| Feature Category | Core Capability | Key Highlights |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
